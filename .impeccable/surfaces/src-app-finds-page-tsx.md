# Ivethskin Finds — public storefront (ivethskin.com)

Scope: src/app/finds (home) and src/app/finds/p/[id] (product). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: US women 25-44 arriving on phones from Pinterest, TikTok and Instagram bio links.
Job: see a find, trust it, tap through to the store. Action: "See it on Amazon/Target/Walmart" (rel sponsored).
Proof: Karen's own photos, label facts, plain disclosure. Constraints: disclosure at top and in footer, no prices, no health claims, own photos only, server-rendered, fast.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every find is a paint chip pulled from a rack: one color family per category, a numbered chip per product. Refuses the cream-and-serif boutique grid and the red-badge deal site.

OWN-WORLD: White rack ground, near-black ink. Eleven category families, each a three-shade strip (pale, tone, deep ink). Chips: photo on top, tone-color body, chip number in tabular figures, short name in a heavy grotesque, small print facts, ink CTA bar. Raises: identical chip module repeated as the ruler (VU bridge); newest chip in the deepest shade (ink basin); every chip numbered (neon program); color laid at full strength, never tinted down (dance poster); facts in a cornered call-out box on the product page (brick instructions); one reserved accent for the active strip (paper automata).

STORY: The visitor sees color, understands each color is a kind of find, picks one, sees real photos and facts, taps to the store knowing it is an affiliate link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Disclosure strip at the very top. Wordmark and one-line headline left. Below, the rack: tall strips, one per category present, each named with its count, filling the width; tapping a strip jumps to its section. On phone the rack scrolls sideways with snap.

FORM: Paint-chip / swatch rack, grounded list position 5, seed key d939e7aa.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
